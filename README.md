# Quantum Tunneling: five-minute visual deck

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. It is offline and needs no installation.

Controls: Right arrow or Space advances; Left arrow goes back; `F` enters fullscreen. Click the right side to advance or the left side to go back.

The deck has 10 scenes, an energy-hill diagram, and a 6.7-second Manim clip. At a natural speaking pace it runs about five minutes.

Use `SPEAKER_NOTES.md` as the talk track. Most animations are native HTML/CSS/Canvas; the tunneling interlude is a locally embedded Manim MP4. Everything remains offline.
